Dataset Overview & Record Schema

Draw Schedule: Tuesday, Thursday, Saturday (18:00–18:30 ICT)

Result Schema: result is an array of 7 integers: the first 6 integers (result[0:6]) are the sorted main winning numbers (drawn from 1 to 55), and the 7th integer (result[6]) is the special/bonus number (used for Jackpot 2).

Technical Notes: Power 6/55 has two cumulative jackpot tiers: Jackpot 1 (matching all 6 main numbers) and Jackpot 2 (matching 5 main numbers plus the 7th special number). When computing main-number vs. bonus-number frequencies separately, slice result[:6] for main balls and result[6] for the special ball.
