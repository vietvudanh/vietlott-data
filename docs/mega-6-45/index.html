Dataset Overview & Record Schema

Draw Schedule: Wednesday, Friday, Sunday (18:00–18:30 ICT)

Result Schema: result is an array of 6 integers: the 6 sorted winning numbers drawn from 1 to 45. Mega 6/45 does NOT have a bonus or special number.

Technical Notes: Unlike Power 6/55 and Power 5/35, Mega 6/45 draws exactly 6 numbers with no 7th bonus ball. The raw filename is data/power645.jsonl for historical compatibility with the internal product config (power_645).
