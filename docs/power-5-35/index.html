Dataset Overview & Record Schema

Draw Schedule: Daily (twice daily draws)

Result Schema: result is an array of 6 integers: the first 5 integers (result[0:5]) are the main winning numbers (drawn from 1 to 35), and the 6th integer (result[5]) is the special number.

Technical Notes: Power 5/35 runs multiple draws per day (so Total Records exceeds Total Draws counted by unique calendar dates). Always separate result[:5] (main numbers) from result[5] (special number) when modeling main vs. special pools.
